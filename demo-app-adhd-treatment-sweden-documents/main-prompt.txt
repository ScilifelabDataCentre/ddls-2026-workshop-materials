Build a small, polished Streamlit application called
"ADHD treatment in Sweden: explore the data".

CONTEXT
This is a public, educational research dashboard for a DDLS workshop.
Use my provided CSV as the only data source. It originates from the
Varden i siffror export of the indicator
"ADHD-behandling bland barn och unga" (data source: Lakemedelsregistret,
Socialstyrelsen). Source page:
https://vardenisiffror.se/sv/indikatorer/adhd-behandling-bland-barn-och-unga

INPUT DATA
File: adhd_treatment_sweden_workshop_2012_2025.csv
Prefer locating the file at data/adhd_treatment_sweden_workshop_2012_2025.csv.
The CSV columns are:
- year: integer year, 2012-2025
- area: "Riket" or the name of one of 21 Swedish regions
- area_type: "National" or "Region"
- treated_per_1000: numeric indicator value

There are 308 rows: 22 areas x 14 years. This is aggregate data,
NOT individual patient-level data. Do not invent observations, retrieve
another dataset, or generate synthetic replacement data. If the CSV is
missing, tell me how to place/upload it instead of silently substituting.

USER INTERFACE
1. Title, short explanation of what the indicator measures, source link,
   and the unit "treated children and young people per 1,000 inhabitants".
2. A sidebar or top panel with:
   - one or more region selectors (default: "Riket"),
   - a year-range control (default: 2012-2025),
   - a year selector for the regional comparison (default: 2025).
3. A line chart of the selected areas' annual rates, with years in order,
   clear axis labels, an accessible legend, and informative tooltips.
4. A bar chart comparing all 21 regions for the selected year, ordered by
   rate; optionally mark the provided "Riket" national value as reference.
5. Simple summary metrics for the selected area and time window, including
   its latest rate and change from the first selected year. Label rate
   differences in "per 1,000", not as percentages.
6. A filterable data table and optional download of the filtered rows.
7. A short limitations section: rates are aggregated; changes do not show
   causation, prevalence, appropriateness of treatment, or individual risk.

VALIDATION AND SCIENTIFIC CARE
- Parse year and treated_per_1000 into numeric types; sort by year.
- Check missing values, repeated area-year pairs, and unexpected columns;

- Verify that "Riket" in 2012 is 16.32 and in 2025 is 48.80.
- Verify the full input contains 308 rows, 22 areas, and 14 years.
- Never calculate "Riket" as the unweighted mean of regional rates;
  use the source's "Riket" observations.
- Do not equate a higher/lower rate with better/worse care.
- Add attribution to the indicator/source and a note to check data
  reuse terms before publishing publicly.
